I am a second-year Ph.D. student at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/), advised by Prof. [Jiaqi Ma](https://jiaqima.github.io/). My research focuses on data-centric machine learning, particularly understanding how training data shape model behavior. I develop scalable training data attribution methods and tools for large language models (LLMs), diffusion models, and reinforcement learning (RL), with the goal of improving data efficiency, interpretability, and trustworthiness.

Before my Ph.D. journey, I received my B.S.E. in Data Science (Summa Cum Laude) from the University of Michigan and B.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Outstanding Graduate) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Jiaqi Ma](https://jiaqima.github.io/), [Todd Hollon](https://mlins.org/), [Honglak Lee](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~honglak/) and [Qiaozhu Mei](https://umich-foreseer.github.io/).
